Dinosaur World - Visit Dinosaurs in all their Glory

Do you or your Kids want to see True to Life and Life sized Dinosaurs. Well, Dinosaur World
is the place to Go. I saw at least 30 different Dinosaurs from Chasmosauraus to Stegosaurus
to Ultrasaurus, T-Rex, you name em. Today, I am highlighting the features of the Park. Over the
next few Days showcase the wildlife that can be viewed there.

"Road of Necks" Click on Picture to View Larger

There's All the adventure of Jurassic Park, But no worries about being gobbled like a Thanksgiving
Day Turkey. All sorts of Dino-entertainment are to be had here. There's a fossil dig,
A Bone Yard, Playground, A Skeleton Garden...
