Shipping Policy

The Awesome Photograph Gallery uses UPS as our primary shipper for all web orders. Our rates are quoted live
through an API connection with UPS Web Services. These are current, live shipping rates and are adjusted by
UPS on a weekly basis.

Orders shipped to APO/FPO and PO Box addresses will be shipped USPS Firstl-Class Mail and NOT insured
for loss or damage. P.O. Box customers please provide us with a physical address if possible, as we cannot
guarantee time or condition of the package.

to most countries. In some circumstances, it does not make sense to charge $110 shipping for a $20 item. We

The Awesome Photograph Gallery is NOT responsible for customs duties, tariffs, VAT or brokerage fees.

We offer three shipping methods for international shipping. We ship to a number of countries, please verify
that we ship to your country before placing your order.

Most orders ship in 5-7 business days. Shipping time excludes holidays and weekends. Orders shipped to
Hawaii and Alaska require UPS 2 Day Air and may take an additional day for delivery.
