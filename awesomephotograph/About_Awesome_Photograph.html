About Jerry Stevens Jr

I am a big believer that the Journey is as important as the destination!
Here's a picture of me hugging the Giant Sequoia
in front of Galen Clarks Cabin in The Mariposa Grove, Yosemite.

Welcome to the personal website where I express my Love of Nature, Photography and
those amazing landmarks. Both natural and Manmade that are a testament to the infinite
world we live on.

My Photographs showcase our natural world and how we humans have reshaped much of
it in our image. Wildlife Photography of the amazing creatures that inhabit our beautiful
world. Twilight and Moonlight Photographs in many famous and not so famous places. To
Photography in America's greatest assets and my Favorite places to Visit our National
Parks. I am simply amazed at all the wondrous places and monuments man has built,
Many are Monuments to the deeds of Men and Hero’s. Others are engineering marvels.

Florida being my home state is the place of some of the most beautiful sunsets and
amazing clouds and weather and a Birding Utopia..Clye Butcher and Ansel Adams being
one of my many inspirational Photographers.

I have traveled many places and have only been a serious photographer since a Trip to
Juneau, Alaska in April / June of 2006 opened my eyes to Photography and Nature as
means to inspire others to open their eyes. My personal need to capture Mother nature
in all her Glory. Hopefully to inspire others to preserve all that can be preserved and to
be gentler with our neighbors in the wild.

I am always Looking Up. To Quote John Muir, Pivotal in the creation of the National Park
System: "Yet it is far safer to wander in God's woods than to travel on black highways or
stay home."
